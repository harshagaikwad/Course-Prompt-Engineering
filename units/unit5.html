UNIT 05 | 06 HOURS

Prompt Safety, Bias, and Ethics

Understand the risks, evaluation methods, and responsible practices associated with Prompt Engineering.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify bias and toxicity in model outputs.
  • Explain misinformation risks.
  • Understand adversarial prompting.
  • Apply safety practices.
  • Understand modern evaluation frameworks.
  • Explain RAG, tool use, and function calling.

Course Topics

1. Bias, Toxicity, and Misinformation

AI systems may generate biased, toxic, inaccurate, or misleading outputs.

2. Adversarial Prompting and Jailbreaks

Adversarial prompts attempt to manipulate models into violating intended restrictions.

3. Safety Best Practices

Good prompt design should include clear constraints, validation, verification, and responsible use.

4. Evaluation Frameworks

Evaluation may include BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, and human evaluation.

5. Future Directions

Important developments include function calling, tool use, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
