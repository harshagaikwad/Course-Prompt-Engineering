UNIT 05 | 06 HOURS

Prompt Safety, Bias, and Ethics

Understand the ethical, social, and safety challenges associated with Large Language Models and Prompt Engineering.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand bias and toxicity in AI outputs.
  • Identify misinformation risks.
  • Understand adversarial prompting and jailbreak attempts.
  • Apply safety best practices.
  • Understand evaluation frameworks.
  • Explain RAG, tool use, and future directions.

Course Topics

1. Bias in Model Outputs

AI systems can reproduce biases present in training data and may generate unfair or stereotypical outputs.

2. Toxicity and Misinformation

Language models may produce harmful, offensive, misleading, or factually incorrect information.

3. Adversarial Prompting and Jailbreaks

Adversarial prompts attempt to manipulate a model into ignoring its intended instructions or safety restrictions.

4. Safety Best Practices

Safe Prompt Engineering includes clear instructions, appropriate constraints, validation, monitoring, and human oversight.

5. Evaluation Frameworks

Outputs can be evaluated using BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, and human evaluation.

6. Function Calling and Tool Use

Modern AI systems can interact with external tools and applications to perform specialized tasks.

7. Retrieval-Augmented Generation

RAG combines information retrieval with generation to provide responses grounded in external knowledge sources.

8. Future Directions

The future of Prompt Engineering includes tool use, agentic systems, multimodal AI, and retrieval-augmented systems.
