Prompt Safety, Bias, and Ethics

Understand the risks associated with AI systems and learn responsible Prompt Engineering practices.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand bias and toxicity in AI outputs.
  • Identify misinformation and hallucination risks.
  • Understand adversarial prompting.
  • Apply safety best practices.
  • Understand AI evaluation frameworks.
  • Explore function calling, tool use, and RAG.

Course Topics

1. Bias and Toxicity

AI models may reproduce biases present in their training data and may generate harmful or inappropriate content.

2. Misinformation and Hallucinations

Models can generate incorrect or unsupported information. Prompt design and evaluation are important for improving reliability.

3. Adversarial Prompting

Adversarial prompts attempt to manipulate model behaviour or bypass safety constraints.

4. Safety Best Practices

Effective safety practices include clear instructions, validation, constraints, human oversight, and output evaluation.

5. Evaluation Frameworks

Evaluation may use BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, human evaluation, accuracy, relevance, and safety criteria.

6. Future Directions

Important developments include function calling, tool use, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
