UNIT 01 | 06 HOURS

Introduction to Prompt Engineering & LLMs

Understand the evolution of NLP, Large Language Models, and the foundations of Prompt Engineering.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain the evolution of NLP to LLMs.
  • Explain tokens, embeddings, and context windows.
  • Describe the importance of Prompt Engineering.
  • Identify major LLM families.
  • Differentiate prompting and fine-tuning.

Course Topics

1. Evolution of NLP to Large Language Models

Natural Language Processing has evolved from rule-based systems and statistical methods to neural networks, transformers, and modern Large Language Models.

2. Anatomy of LLMs

Important components include tokens, embeddings, attention mechanisms, parameters, and context windows.

3. Why Prompt Engineering Matters

Prompt Engineering helps users communicate effectively with AI models and obtain reliable, relevant, and useful outputs.

4. Applications in AI/ML Systems

Prompt Engineering is used in education, coding, content generation, data analysis, chatbots, research, and automation.

5. Introduction to Major LLMs

Examples include GPT, Claude, PaLM, Gemini, Mistral, and other modern language models.

6. Prompting vs Fine-Tuning

Prompting modifies the instructions given to a model, whereas fine-tuning adapts model parameters using additional training data.
