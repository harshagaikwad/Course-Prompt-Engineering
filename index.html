Learn Prompt Engineering from Foundations to Advanced Applications

This course covers the evolution of Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, prompt design, advanced prompting techniques, domain-specific applications, safety, bias, ethics, evaluation, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
